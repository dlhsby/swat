# Change Password

The Change Password page lets you update your account password. There are two scenarios:
voluntary change anytime, and mandatory change on first sign-in.

## Voluntary Password Change

To change your password anytime:

1. Open the avatar menu in the top-right corner → **Profile**.
2. In the **Security** card, click the **Change Password** button.
3. Enter your **Current Password**.
4. Enter your **New Password** that meets the requirements (see below).
5. Enter **Confirm New Password** (must match your new password).
6. Click **Save Password**.

If successful, you'll return to the [dashboard](/memulai/dashboard) and see a confirmation message.

## Password Requirements

Your new password must:

- **Be at least 12 characters long**
- Contain **uppercase letters** (A–Z)
- Contain **lowercase letters** (a–z)
- Contain **numbers** (0–9)
- Contain **special symbols** (!@#$%^&\* etc.)

The page displays a **strength meter** that shows how strong your password is:

- 🔴 **Very weak**
- 🟠 **Weak**
- 🟡 **Medium**
- 🟢 **Strong**
- 🟢 **Very strong**

The **Save Password** button only becomes active when your password meets all requirements.

## Mandatory Password Change (First Sign-In)

Some accounts are required to change their password on first sign-in for security:

1. After entering your username and password on the [sign-in page](/memulai/login),
   you'll be automatically sent to the **Change Password** page.
2. A yellow alert reminds you: _"For security, you must change your password before continuing."_
3. For mandatory changes, **you don't need to enter your current password** — just enter
   your new password and confirmation.
4. Click **Change & Continue**.
5. You'll then land on the [dashboard](/memulai/dashboard).

:::note Access is blocked until password is changed
If your account requires a mandatory password change, you can't enter the application
until you complete it. The **Sign Out** button on this page lets you return to the
sign-in page if needed.
:::

## Forgot Your Password?

If you forgot your current password:

- Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page.
- Ask your administrator to reset your password.

After your administrator resets it, you'll sign in with a temporary password, and the
system will ask you to change it on first sign-in (just like the **Mandatory Change**
scenario above).
