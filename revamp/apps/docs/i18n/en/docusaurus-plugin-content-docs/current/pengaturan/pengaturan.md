# Settings

![SWAT Settings page](/img/web/settings.png)

The Settings page lets you customize the application's appearance and preferences.

## Appearance

Choose your interface theme:

- **System** — follows your device's theme setting (light or dark).
- **Light** — always use the light theme.
- **Dark** — always use the dark theme.

Your choice is saved automatically in your browser.

## Language

Choose the application interface language:

- **Bahasa Indonesia** (id-ID)
- **English** (en-US)

Once you select a language, all menu labels, buttons, and messages change accordingly.
Your choice is saved automatically.

## Tracking (GPS)

If you have permission to manage deviation rules, the **Tracking** section will appear.
Here you can adjust GPS parameters:

- **Corridor distance** — tolerance for route deviation from the planned path.
- **Stop duration** — how long a vehicle may stop without being considered off-route.
- **Delay** — time tolerance for completing hauls.

:::note Special permission required
This section only appears if your account has `deviation-rule:manage` permission (or equivalent).
Contact your administrator if you think you should have access to these settings.
:::

## About

The **About** section displays general application information:

- Application name (SWAT)
- Operating organization
- Current application version
