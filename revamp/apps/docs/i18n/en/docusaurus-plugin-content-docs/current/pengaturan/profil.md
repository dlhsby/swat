# Profile

![SWAT Profile page](/img/web/profile.png)

The Profile page displays your account information and provides controls for security and session management.

## Account Information

The **Account Information** card shows your profile details:

- **Avatar** — profile photo (if available) or name initials.
- **Full Name** — the name registered in the system.
- **Username** — your unique identifier (starts with `@`).
- **Role** — your role in the system (for example: Admin, Checker, Operator, etc.).

This information is displayed from your account record and cannot be changed on this page.
Contact your administrator if you need to update your profile information.

## Security

The **Security** card lets you manage your password:

1. Click the **Change Password** button.
2. You'll be sent to the [Change Password](/pengaturan/ganti-kata-sandi) page.
3. Enter your current password, new password, and confirmation.

:::tip Change your password regularly
To keep your account secure, it's recommended to change your password periodically
(for example, every 3 months).
:::

## Session

The **Session** card provides control over your current device:

1. Click the **Logout** button to end your session and return to the sign-in page.
2. You'll need to sign in again with your username and password to continue.

:::note One session per device
The **Logout** button only ends the session on your current device. If you're signed in
from another device, that session will remain active.
:::
